IMPORTANT POINTS

Commission is paid on the total weekly agency target in Red Diamonds.

  • Below 3M Red Diamonds: Agency receives 10% commission.
  • Above 3M Red Diamonds: Agency receives 15% commission.
  • Above 10M Red Diamonds: Agency receives 20% commission.

EXAMPLE

Total Agency Target: 6M Red Diamonds

Agency Commission: 6M × 15% = 900K Red Diamonds
